      How it rules

      Scripts measure and decide. A fact the scripts could not measure makes the ruling “unable to rule”, and the ruling names that fact. Each Meta model is named where it is used.

            Two aisle clips from free-licence warehouse video, faces blurred.

            The aisle camera sits near the floor at the end of the aisle. The judge reads the camera's height above the floor from its four floor marks alone, before any model call, and . Under the line, every rule that reads the truck's floor position (separation, spotter, structure contact and speed) is unable to rule, naming camera.height. And the hi-vis rule reads the torso, not the floor, and is still ruled.

            A three-run model read, in the optional words beside a ruling, took a person in a pink shirt for someone with no hi-vis. That changes no verdict, and it shows why a model's words never decide one.
