# Experiments

Each experiment here has one log: the question, how it ran, what it cost, what was found and what was decided.
Every paid call ran through the metering proxy, under a spend cap, except two sub-cent test calls noted in the Spark Open log.
The tooling named in the logs is private and is described in [PROVENANCE.md](../PROVENANCE.md).

| Date | Experiment | Question | Headline figure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-26 | [Capability probe](2026-10-26-capability-probe/LOG.md) | What can Meta's models do on each candidate steward feature? | All 29 probes that can run have run: 15 worked, 14 worked badly; top score 0.75, a local uncertainty signal for the router |
| 2026-10-26 | [Steward limits](2026-10-26-steward-limits/LOG.md) | Given a driver's incident report and the evidence clip, can a video-only steward work out who is who, find the incident, rule correctly and show evidence a human steward can check? | First batch: 18 right, 4 wrong, 3 not scorable for $1.998; second batch: 18 right, 5 wrong, 10 not scorable for $5.14 |
| 2026-10-26 | [Router tier 2: Spark Open](2026-10-26-router-spark-open/LOG.md) | Can Muse Spark Open replace `muse-spark-1.3` as the router's tier 2 without losing answer quality? | Spark Open passed 49 of 55 (89.1%) against Spark 1.3's 50 of 55 (90.9%), at about the same cost per call |

| 2026-10-27 | [Smoke test D: forklift locator](2026-10-27-smoke-d-forklift-locator/LOG.md) | Can a script walk a long warehouse clip with no report and return a short list of active windows, the same way every run? | 28 of 28 active spans found on a 421 s joined shift, 0 windows in the idle gaps, byte-identical on two runs; $0 |
| 2026-10-27 | [Smoke test E: hi-vis colour rule](2026-10-27-smoke-e-hi-vis/LOG.md) | Can a script read a person's vest from colour alone, in three states, without ever calling a vested person unvested? | On 16 fixed-camera clips 0 of 9 vested people read unvested and 4 of 4 unvested people were found; on 19 moving-camera clips one washed-out vest read unvested, which fails the mark; $0 |
| 2026-10-27 | [Smoke test F: reversing and spotter](2026-10-27-smoke-f-reversing-spotter/LOG.md) | Can a script read reversing travel and a spotter in the rear cone from truck and fork-end boxes? | 2 of 3 clips met the mark; the clip labelled "reversing with a spotter" shows the truck travelling forward; $0.04 of SAM |

| 2026-10-27 | [Smoke test H: detector and one ruling](2026-10-27-smoke-h-detector-ruling/LOG.md) | Does a pinned person detector count people right, and does one Spark Open ruling per window rule under the right rule? | DETR counted 15 of 16 clips right; the ruling did not meet its mark: 2 of 3 compliant windows got a false separation breach and 2 of 3 breach windows hit the token ceiling; $0.1413 of Spark Open |
