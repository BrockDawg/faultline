title: Wear high-visibility clothing in a truck zone
---
Anyone on foot inside a truck-operating zone wears high-visibility clothing.

On video: A person inside a marked truck zone has no fluorescent yellow-green or orange-red band on the torso. A person who is too small to read, cut by the frame edge, mostly hidden or in low light is not ruled.

Source: Site rule
