title: Do not touch racking or other structures
---
A truck or its load never touches racking, uprights, guards, columns or stacked stock.

On video: The truck or its load meets racking, an upright, a guard, a column or stacked stock. A consequence within half a second (the structure shifts, something falls, the truck stops abruptly) is the evidence that the touch was real; a touch with no visible consequence is still a breach of this rule.

Source: Site rule
