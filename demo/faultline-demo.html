A rules judge for video

faultline

A site's rules pack and its camera footage go in. Rulings come out, each with the rule, the frames and the number that decided it.

Scripts measure and decide. A fact the scripts could not measure makes the ruling “unable to rule”, and the ruling names that fact.

The rules pack, as the site wrote it

4 rules, one file each, in plain words

  1. Wear high-visibility clothing in a truck zone

    Anyone on foot inside a truck-operating zone wears high-visibility clothing.

    On video: A person inside a marked truck zone has no fluorescent yellow-green or orange-red band on the torso. A person who is too small to read, cut by the frame edge, mostly hidden or in low light is not ruled.

  2. Reverse with a spotter where the site requires one

    Where the site requires a spotter, a truck reverses only with a spotter in high-visibility clothing watching the rear path. The site requires one at [a blind zone / a dock / a load that blocks the view].

    On video: Inside such a zone, the truck travels against its fork end for a second or more with no vested person behind it.

  3. Keep the site distance between a moving truck and a person on foot

    A moving truck keeps at least [3] metres from any person on foot. The site's own value replaces the placeholder.

    On video: A person's feet come within the site distance of a moving truck's footprint, with the site's own distance.

  4. Do not touch racking or other structures

    A truck or its load never touches racking, uprights, guards, columns or stacked stock.

    On video: The truck or its load meets racking, an upright, a guard, a column or stacked stock. A consequence within half a second (the structure shifts, something falls, the truck stops abruptly) is the evidence that the touch was real; a touch with no visible consequence is still a breach of this rule.

Three verdicts, never a guess

Every rule gets one of three answers

Breach

the rule was broken, and the measured number that decided it is shown

Clear

the rule held, measured on the frames

Unable to rule

a fact was not measured, and the ruling names it
