---
id: no_forward_tilt_when_raised
title: Do not tilt a raised load forward except over the rack
---
Tilt with great care, above all when stacking high. With the forks raised, tilt forward only to pick up a load. A raised load tilts forward only when it sits in place over a rack or stack. When stacking, use only enough back tilt to steady the load.

On video: The mast tilts forward with the load high and away from the rack, or the load slides off a raised, forward-tilted fork.

Source: 1910.178(o)(6)
