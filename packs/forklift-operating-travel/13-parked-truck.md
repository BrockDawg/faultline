title: Leave a parked truck safe
---
If the operator leaves the truck, the forks go fully down and the controls go to neutral, with the brakes set. If the operator goes 25 feet or more away, or out of sight, the power also goes off. If it is left unattended on a slope, the wheels are blocked.

On video: The operator walks away from a truck with the forks raised, or the truck rolls after the operator leaves it. A truck left out of the operator's sight still has its power on.

Source: 1910.178(m)(5)(i), 1910.178(m)(5)(ii), 1910.178(m)(5)(iii)
