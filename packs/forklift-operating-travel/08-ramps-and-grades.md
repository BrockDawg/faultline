id: ramps_and_grades
title: Take grades slowly, with the load upgrade and kept low
---
Go up and down slopes slowly. On grades over 10 percent, a loaded truck keeps its load on the uphill side. On any grade, tilt the load back where the truck allows it, and raise it only enough to clear the ground.

On video: A loaded truck goes down a steep ramp (over 10 percent) forks first, or climbs one with the load on the downhill side. The forks are carried high on a slope, or the truck moves fast on it.

Source: 1910.178(n)(7), 1910.178(n)(7)(i), 1910.178(n)(7)(iii)
