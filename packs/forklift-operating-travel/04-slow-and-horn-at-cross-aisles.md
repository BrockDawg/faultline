id: slow_and_horn_at_cross_aisles
title: Slow down and sound the horn where the view is blocked
---
At cross aisles, doorways and other places where the operator cannot see what is coming, slow down and sound the horn before going through.

On video: The truck goes through a cross aisle, doorway or blind corner without slowing. If the clip has sound, no horn is heard before the truck enters the blind spot.

Source: 1910.178(n)(4)
