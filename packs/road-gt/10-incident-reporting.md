title: Incident Reporting
---
• File incidents through the official report form — it's the **only** way to open a steward review.
• Reports may be filed by [the drivers involved / any member who witnessed it].
• Submit within [48] hours of the race ending.
• Include the drivers involved, the lap number, a short description, and a video clip or timestamp.
• Do not litigate incidents in public chat — let the stewards review.
