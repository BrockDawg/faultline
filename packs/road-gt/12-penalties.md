title: Penalties
---
• In-race penalties (sim or admin): warning, drive-through, stop-and-go, or disqualification.
• Post-race penalties: time added, grid drop next race, points deduction, or suspension.
• Penalty points accumulate over a rolling [8]-race window: [4 = qualifying ban, 6 = pit-lane start, 8 = race ban].
• Egregious or repeat offenses escalate to probation, suspension, or removal at staff discretion.
