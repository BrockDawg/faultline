title: Racecraft & Passing
---
• The car attempting the pass is responsible for completing it safely.
• Leave racing room — at least a car's width — when a driver is alongside.
• Only one defensive move per corner, and never move in the braking zone.
• No dive-bombs, no slide-jobs that end in contact, no using the bumper to move a car.
• If you complete a pass by making contact, give the position back.
