title: General Conduct
---
• Treat every member with respect, on track and in chat — clean racecraft is the baseline expectation.
• **No retaliation, ever.** If someone wrecks you, bring it to the stewards — do not race-back.
• No intentional wrecking, brake-checking, or causing a deliberate caution.
• Hard racing and honest mistakes happen; do not accuse others of malice over a racing incident.
• Own your mistakes — if you caused contact, apologize and move on.
