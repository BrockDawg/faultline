id: trailer_secured
title: Secure the trailer or truck before driving into it
---
Before a forklift drives into a highway truck or trailer, the trailer's brakes are set and its wheels chocked. A trailer with no tractor attached may need fixed jacks. The trailer floor is checked for breaks and weak spots before the forklift drives onto it.

On video: The trailer moves away from the dock, tips, or its floor breaks while a forklift is inside. No chocks or restraint are visible on the trailer wheels.

Source: 1910.178(k)(1), 1910.178(k)(3), 1910.178(m)(7)
