id: elevator_entry
title: Enter lifts slowly and squarely, then shut down
---
Approach a goods lift slowly and enter it squarely once its floor is level. Inside, set the controls to neutral, switch off and set the brakes. A motorised hand truck enters a lift or tight space load first.

On video: The truck enters a lift fast, at an angle, or before the floor is level. The truck stays powered while the lift moves.

Source: 1910.178(n)(12), 1910.178(n)(13)
