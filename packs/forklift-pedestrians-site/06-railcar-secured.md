id: railcar_secured
title: Secure the rail car before loading
---
A rail car is blocked from moving while it is loaded or unloaded, and while a dockboard or bridge plate is in place.

On video: A rail car moves during loading, or while a bridge plate still spans the gap.

Source: 1910.178(k)(2), 1910.178(k)(4), 1910.178(m)(7)
