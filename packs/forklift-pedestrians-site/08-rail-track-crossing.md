---
id: rail_track_crossing
title: Cross rail tracks at an angle and do not park near them
---
Where possible, cross rail tracks diagonally. Do not park closer than 8 feet from the centre of a rail track.

On video: The truck crosses rails square on when an angle was possible, or is parked beside the track clearly closer than 8 feet to its centre.

Source: 1910.178(n)(5)
