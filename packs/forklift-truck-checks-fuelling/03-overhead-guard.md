id: overhead_guard
title: Use the overhead guard
---
A high-lift rider truck has an overhead guard fitted and in place, unless operating conditions do not permit one. The guard protects the operator from small falling objects. It is not built to stop a full falling load.

On video: A high-lift rider truck works with its overhead guard missing or removed.

Source: 1910.178(m)(9), 1910.178(e)(1)
