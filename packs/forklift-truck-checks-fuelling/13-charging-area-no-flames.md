id: charging_area_no_flames
title: No smoking, flames or sparks in the charging area
---
Batteries charge only in the area set aside for it. No one smokes there, and open flames, sparks and electric arcs are kept away.

On video: Someone smokes, welds, grinds or uses a flame near charging batteries, or a battery charges outside the marked charging area.

Source: 1910.178(g)(1), 1910.178(g)(10), 1910.178(g)(11)
