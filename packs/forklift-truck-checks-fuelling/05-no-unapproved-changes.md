---
id: no_unapproved_changes
title: Do not alter the truck or add counterweight
---
Do not add, remove or move parts of the truck beyond the maker's design. Do not add counterweight unless the maker approves it.

On video: Extra weight, or people, sit on the counterweight to balance a load. Home-made fork extensions or other added parts are in use.

Source: 1910.178(q)(6)
