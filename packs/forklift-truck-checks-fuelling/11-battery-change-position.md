---
id: battery_change_position
title: Park and brake the truck before changing or charging its battery
---
Before a battery is changed or charged, the truck is parked in position with its brake set. A refitted battery is placed and secured properly.

On video: A battery is changed or put on charge while the truck can roll, or a refitted battery sits loose or shifts.

Source: 1910.178(g)(8), 1910.178(g)(5)
