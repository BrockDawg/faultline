id: unsafe_truck_out_of_service
title: Take an unsafe truck out of service
---
A truck that needs repair, is defective or is unsafe in any way is taken out of service until it is safe again. Only authorised people make repairs.

On video: The truck keeps working with visible damage, a broken part, a leak or a fault that shows in how it moves.

Source: 1910.178(p)(1), 1910.178(q)(1)
