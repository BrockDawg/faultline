---
id: electrolyte_mixing
title: Pour acid into water, never water into acid
---
When electrolyte is mixed, acid is poured into water. Water is never poured into acid. A carboy tilter or siphon is provided for handling electrolyte.

On video: Water is poured into a container of acid, or electrolyte is poured by hand from a carboy, with no tilter or siphon in view.

Source: 1910.178(g)(7), 1910.178(g)(6)
