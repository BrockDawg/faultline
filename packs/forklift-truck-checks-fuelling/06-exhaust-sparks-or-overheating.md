id: exhaust_sparks_or_overheating
title: Stop a truck that throws sparks or overheats
---
A truck that throws sparks or flames from its exhaust, or runs so hot that it becomes a hazard, is taken out of service until the cause is fixed.

On video: Sparks or flames come from the exhaust, or smoke or steam comes from the engine or motor, and the truck keeps working.

Source: 1910.178(q)(8), 1910.178(q)(9)
