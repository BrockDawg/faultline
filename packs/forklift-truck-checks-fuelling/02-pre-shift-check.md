id: pre_shift_check
title: Check the truck before it is used
---
Each truck is checked before use, at least once a day, or after every shift if it runs round the clock. Faults found are reported and fixed.

On video: Judge only if the clip shows the start of a shift. The truck goes to work with a fault a check would plainly catch, such as a flat tyre, a missing guard or a hydraulic leak.

Source: 1910.178(q)(7)
