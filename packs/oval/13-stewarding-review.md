title: Stewarding & Review
---
• Stewards review the clip and classify the incident: racing incident (no fault), avoidable (majority fault), or shared fault.
• Overlap test: if the attacking car's front axle is alongside the defender's rear axle at turn-in, both must leave room.
• A racing incident — hard but fair, or an unavoidable chain reaction — carries no penalty.
• Decisions are made by a [majority of the steward panel]; involved staff recuse themselves.
