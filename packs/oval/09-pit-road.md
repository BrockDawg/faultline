title: Pit Road
---
• Pit entry and exit lanes are set before each race and **must** be followed.
• Stay in the outer lane until within [3] stalls of your box; blend back out the same way.
• Do not deliberately slow on pit road to manipulate your restart lane.
• If you can't get your stop in time and must race back, alert the field — P2 becomes leader and sets the restart.
