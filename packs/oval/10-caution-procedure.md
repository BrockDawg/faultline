• Cautions are [sim-controlled / called manually by race control for multi-car incidents].
• When the yellow flies the field freezes — hold position at the leader's pace, no racing back.
• The first car one lap down receives the free pass (lucky dog) — toggled per series.
• If you're at fault, claim it in [incident channel] before two-to-go to reduce your penalty.
