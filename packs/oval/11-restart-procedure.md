title: Restart Procedure
---
• Restarts are double-file unless called otherwise; lapped cars line up at the rear.
• The leader controls the restart and chooses a lane; no passing before the start/finish line.
• No laying back to get a run, no lane changes, and no weaving to warm tires on the pace lap.
• Choose rule is [enabled / disabled] — drivers pick a lane in Turn [2].
