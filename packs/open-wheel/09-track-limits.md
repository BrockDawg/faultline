title: Track Limits
---
• Keep at least two wheels within the white lines; all four wheels beyond them is a violation.
• No penalty for a clean run-off where no time or position is gained.
• Repeat offenses escalate: [1st = warning, 2nd = +5s, 3rd = drive-through], reset each race.
• Persistent abuse after the limit is reached may be reviewed by the stewards.
